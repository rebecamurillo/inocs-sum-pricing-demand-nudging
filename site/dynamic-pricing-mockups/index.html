Mockups for your review

We are building a new dynamic pricing demonstrator. It runs the pricing model as its author wrote it, on a part of Geneva, and shows what the model's prices do to how people travel. These are the design mockups. Nothing is built yet, so this is the cheapest moment to change anything.

Every number, name and map is sample data. Text on a yellow ground, like [Q-17], marks wording or a figure that waits for an answer, most of them from you.
