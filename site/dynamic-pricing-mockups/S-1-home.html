For cities and public transport operators

Price shared bikes by the trip, and fewer people take the car.

A research model from the SUM project sets the bike price of each trip, to nudge travellers from the car to shared bikes and public transport.

  1. 1
    The problemFor each trip, people compare time, price and changes. Too often the car wins, and a flat bike price is the same on every trip.
  2. 2
    The ideaYou set a minimum and a maximum. The model chooses a price for each trip, lower where a bike can replace a car. Nobody is forced: travellers still choose. [Q-28]
  3. 3
    A familiar lever, a different goalPlane tickets move with demand to fill the plane. Here the price moves with the trip, to take cars off the street. [comparison to confirm]
